LAKESIDE PAVILION ANNOUNCES EMERGENCY BRIDGE REPAIRS & TEMPORARY CLOSURE
Lakeside Pavilion & Gardens announced today that due to structural issues discovered on the primary access bridge, the entire facility will be closed for all events from late October through mid-November while repairs are underway. All reservation holders for this period are being contacted for cancellations and full refunds.
